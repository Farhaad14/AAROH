# Demo Instructions

## Hackathon Demo Walkthrough Scenario

1. **Open AAROH**: Navigate to `http://localhost:3000`.
2. **Launch Noida Demo Route**: Click the preset button **"Sector 62 → Sector 18"**.
3. **Analyze 6:00 PM Context**:
   - Observe that Route 1 has a high score (84/100) due to open commercial shops, active transit, and ambient lighting.
4. **Time Comparison Demo (11:30 PM)**:
   - Click the **"11:30 PM"** time toggle button in the header bar.
   - Watch the instant recalculation: commercial shop scores drop as operating hours close, transit schedule availability decreases, isolation index increases for sector roads, and street lighting weight becomes critical.
   - Observe that Route 2 (Main Expressway Arterial) becomes recommended over sector roads because of continuous streetlight density and 24/7 fuel/police presence.
5. **Attention Zones Inspection**:
   - Click an Attention Zone marker on the map to open the Segment Inspector modal and review specific reasons (e.g. "Low activity proxy", "Few open businesses").
6. **Field Observation Submission**:
   - Click "Submit Field Observation" to add a user lighting report and watch live score updates.
